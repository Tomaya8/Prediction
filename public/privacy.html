Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, and password (stored as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from those providers.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the App, including trades, tournament entries, predictions, and credit transactions.

Device Information: We collect device type, operating system, and push notification tokens to deliver notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's features (trading, tournaments, leaderboards)
  • To process in-app purchases and manage your credit balance
  • To send push notifications about tournament results, market updates, and rewards
  • To display leaderboards and user profiles to other players
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms
  • To improve the App and develop new features

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Other Users: Your username, credit balance, trade statistics, and tournament rankings are visible to other users on leaderboards and profiles.
  • Service Providers: Firebase (hosting, database, authentication), RevenueCat (in-app purchases), Expo (push notifications), CoinGecko and ExchangeRate API (market data).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or legal process.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) servers. We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted password storage (bcrypt hashing)
  • JWT authentication with expiring tokens
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • CORS restrictions on API access

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your profile and transaction history in the App
  • Update: Change your username and password in Settings
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all associated data via Settings > Delete Account
  • Opt Out: Disable push notifications and email notifications in Settings

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed from our systems, including trades, holdings, transactions, and social connections.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

9. Push Notifications

We send push notifications for tournament results, daily rewards, market resolutions, and referral rewards. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device settings or the App's Settings page.

10. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand App usage patterns. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: support@predich.app
